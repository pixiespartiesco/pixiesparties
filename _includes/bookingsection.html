Booking & Pricing Policies

  • Guest Limits: Classic ($250 CAD, 60 min) and Deluxe ($350 CAD, 90 min) packages include supplies for up to 10 children. Additional children are $10 CAD each.

  • Service Areas & Travel: Serving Calgary, Airdrie, Cochrane, Okotoks, and surrounding Southern Alberta communities. Events outside Calgary city limits may incur a travel fee.
